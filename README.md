## **Yalin Yang**
#### Geospatial Data Science: the science that investigates the 'where' and 'why' of various human and natural phenomena!

+ I am a Research Associate and GIS Programmer at the [West Virginia GIS Technical Center](https://wvgis.wvu.edu/) at West Virginia University. I hold a Ph.D. in Geospatial Information Sciences from the University of Texas at Dallas (德州大学达拉斯分校). My research interests include Geographic Information Science (GIS), Urban Environment, and Human Networks Modeling. Specifically, I apply geospatial big data, machine learning, and cloud computing (e.g. AWS) to study the interaction among built environments and social events, semantic segmentation of urban areas, and navigation with map matching. I am also passionate about developing helpful applications for local communities.
